# Third-party notices: @luma.gl/experimental

Experimental rendering, materials, simulation, and model helpers by vis.gl contributors.
RAPIDS-inspired analysis operations have moved to the graph package and are credited
in its notice index. Scientific flow examples implement GPU field sampling and advection;
their numerical models and approximations are described in the feature documentation.
