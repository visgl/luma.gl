# Example credits and notices

Example code, assets, fonts, and datasets have distinct provenance. Preserve
these notices alongside standalone distributions.

## PicoGL.js examples

Tarek Sherif and PicoGL.js contributors supplied the examples ported in
`api/texture-3d/app.ts` and `showcase/dof/app.ts`. The original source links are
retained in those files. The historical import revisions are unknown.
The ports adapt resource creation, shader bindings, animation, and rendering to
luma.gl. Full upstream terms: [PicoGL.js MIT license](./licenses/picogl.txt).
