# OpenUSD example assets

## Mini vehicles: CC0

The files under `mini-vehicles/` are selected from the USD Working Group's
`full_assets/Vehicles/USD_Mini_Car_Kit` collection:

The bundled subset includes the referenced `textures/global-colors/*.jpg` and
`textures/global-textures/*.jpg` image assets used by the retained USD materials.

https://github.com/usd-wg/assets/tree/main/full_assets/Vehicles

The collection is dedicated to the public domain under Creative Commons Zero 1.0:

https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Original 3D assets: Kenney, https://kenney.nl/

USD conversion and assembly: Robin-Yann Storm, https://rystorm.com/

## Utah and Fancy teapots: CC0

The files under `teapots/` are selected from the USD Working Group's `full_assets/Teapot/geo`
collection:

https://github.com/usd-wg/assets/tree/main/full_assets/Teapot

The teapot asset is dedicated to the public domain under Creative Commons Zero 1.0.
Its USD assembly was authored by Chris Rydalch at SideFX Software. The original Fancy Teapot was
created by James Ray Cook, Jurita Burger, and Rico Cilliers at PolyHaven:

https://polyhaven.com/a/tea_set_01
