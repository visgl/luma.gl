About this prototype

Free practice: pot any ball, in any order. A scratched cue ball returns after the shot. Physics stays on the GPU; a small snapshot checks pockets and resting balls. Shots are capped at 12 table units/s. Contact offset applies follow, draw or sidespin. Cloth friction converts sliding to rolling; cushions exchange spin at a raised contact and lose more rebound pace on harder impacts. Choose a practice setup to compare shots. Throw toggles isolate linear and angular surface-slip contributions to ball friction; these are experimental approximations. Continuous collision detection is future work.
