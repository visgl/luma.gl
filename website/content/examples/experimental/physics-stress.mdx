Select a stress scenario, compare solvers and timesteps, and export a report containing
completion latency, recovery attempts, buffer budgets, compact contact workload and final poses.
Sparse-contact, rotated-geometry and sustained-stack scenarios supplement the original fixtures. The diagnostic
front projection reads GPU poses back to the CPU; these timings are not rendering benchmarks.

The **Box3D comparison** scenarios use the same authored inputs as the matched native
CPU/WebGPU benchmark suite: box piles, mixed sizes, stacks, joint chains, rotating fast
bars and ballistic motion. The live view is for diagnosis. The command-line runner
measures fresh worlds without rendering and reports quality separately from performance;
see the [benchmark protocol](https://github.com/visgl/luma.gl/blob/master/scripts/physics/matched/README.md).
