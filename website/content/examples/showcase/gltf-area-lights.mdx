The Modern Living Room is by Wig42, licensed [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
Scene conversion by Benedikt Bitterli; the glTF conversion is loaded from the pinned
Foundation Resources source repository, including its original area-light records.
Geometry is about 33 MB, plus textures delivered from the repository's Git LFS media endpoints.

This experimental profile uses sixteen surface samples per light. Direct PBR
illumination supports rectangles and disks; shadows and indirect bounce are not simulated.
