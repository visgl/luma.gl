Drag to orbit an animated sculpture with three deforming rings, a glowing core,
iridescent lighting, and a floor reflection. Try the material, twist, and exposure
controls, or pause to inspect the surfaces. No mesh is uploaded: the fragment shader
traces camera rays through a signed-distance field.

The application registers the experimental TypeScript Slang compiler with its own shader
assembler. Both backends use the same `shader.slang`, reusable `geometry.slang`
distance-field module, and `palette.slang` material module. Animation and camera
parameters share a flat uniform buffer; the application uses compiler reflection to
bind its resource by the generated name.
WebGL 2 receives GLSL ES 300; WebGPU receives one WGSL program with generated entry points.
See the [Slang API](/docs/api-reference/slang) for compiler limits.
