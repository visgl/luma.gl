Select **Tip first tile** and watch the teal and orange branches split around the center.
Both rows converge on a wide receiver that starts the gold finish. Drag to orbit or scroll to zoom.
Reset restores the starting layout while keeping your camera.

luma.gl advances the rigid bodies on the GPU. Three.js renders instanced meshes using
the published matrices, packed into vertex buffers on the same WebGPU device with
[ThreeBufferBridge](/docs/api-reference/three). The only input to the cascade is the impulse
on the first tile. Contacts carry the motion through both junctions.
