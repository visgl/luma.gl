The Three.js app owns the renderer, scene, and orbit controls. Add deck.gl paths and points using an overlay, or share depth with opaque Three objects in WebGL2. Switch projection, composition, and resolution to compare behavior. The WebGPU host currently supports overlay composition; shared depth is available with the WebGL2 host.
